Frequently Asked Questions

How long does delivery take inside Kathmandu Valley? ▼
Standard delivery inside Kathmandu, Lalitpur, and Bhaktapur takes 24 to 48 hours immediately after our roasting process is completed.
How should I store my coffee beans to keep them fresh? ▼
Keep your beans in an airtight, opaque container away from direct sunlight, heat, and moisture. Avoid storing them in the refrigerator, as condensation can ruin the flavor profile.
Are your coffee beans sourced ethically? ▼
Yes! All our specialty coffee beans are sourced directly from partner farms in Nepal and ethical micro-lots globally, ensuring fair wages and sustainable farming practices.
Can I customize my coffee subscription delivery dates? ▼
Absolutely. You can easily modify your delivery frequency, pause a shipment, or switch coffee flavor profiles anytime through your account profile or by contacting our team.
