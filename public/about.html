Our Mission

We believe coffee connects humanity

Bringing the Brew to You and Yours

What started as a shared vision between childhood friends in Kathmandu is now a global connection linking local Himalayan estates and remote African micro-lots. We skip institutional traders entirely to guarantee raw product quality while injecting fair compensation back to the families who sweat in volcanic fields.

Every bean is manually cupped for defects before being cataloged in temperature-controlled bags. We roast on custom-built air ovens within hours of delivery ensuring you smell the mountain rain in every cup.
